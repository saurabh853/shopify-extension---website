Export Print on Demand Catalog Data

Learn how to cleanly parse and download variant listings from target stores.

If you are operating in the print on demand industry, performing competitor research or migration tasks is essential. Scraping product listings for custom graphic hoodies and printed ceramic mugs allows you to analyze market positioning and product setups. With the Shopify Product Exporter Chrome extension, you can export these catalogs locally without having to worry about complex print template file sizes, mock-up images, base colors, and print size ranges. This guide details how to extract catalog lists, parse design IDs, shirt sizes, base colors, and variant SKU maps, and map product data for your own store.

Key Niche Capabilities

  • Preserve Variant Relationships: Automatically maps complex product options (like sizing grids, material types, and colorways) into structured columns so you can import them into your catalog without losing parent-child structures.
  • Extract High-Resolution CDN Images: Download image URLs for the entire Print on Demand store catalog, including high-res zoom imagery, variant-specific images, and thumbnail assets.
  • Handle Complex Metadata: Correctly parses design IDs, shirt sizes, base colors, and variant SKU maps fields, ensuring prices, inventories, and custom tags are captured with absolute integrity.
  • Bypass Sizing & Dimension Limits: Seamlessly scraper thousands of items (such as custom graphic hoodies and printed ceramic mugs) across multiple collections without encountering timeouts or getting rate-limited.

3-Step Export Guide for Print on Demand Stores

  1. Install the Shopify Product Exporter Chrome Extension: Add the extension to Chrome in one click. Open the extension panel and pin it to your browser toolbar for quick, instant access whenever you are browsing Print on Demand shops.
  2. Visit the Target Print on Demand Shopify Store: Navigate to the storefront you want to analyze or migrate. The extension automatically scans the page metadata, validates the window structure, and highlights green once a Shopify-powered store is detected.
  3. Extract and Download the Catalog: Click the Shopify Product Exporter icon. Select your output format: CSV (native Shopify format for direct re-imports), Excel (.xlsx) (styled sheets with option worksheets), or JSON (developer format).
