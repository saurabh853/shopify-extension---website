How to Use This Directory

Choose the Right Shopify Export Workflow

Each niche page explains which product fields, variant patterns, and catalog checks matter before you export Shopify products to CSV, Excel, or JSON.

Use this directory when you need a Shopify product scraper workflow for a specific market. A clothing catalog needs size and color checks, a beauty catalog needs shade and ingredient review, and an electronics catalog often depends on model numbers, bundles, and compatibility notes.

Start with the niche closest to the store you are studying, export the catalog locally, then compare product identity fields, variant fields, pricing fields, and image coverage. For general format help, visit the CSV export guide, Excel export guide, or Shopify competitor research workflow.
