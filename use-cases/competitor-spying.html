The Best Seller Query Hack

By default, many Shopify storefronts allow you to sort any collection by sales volume. Append the query parameter `?sort_by=best-selling` to the end of any Shopify collection URL:

https://competitorstore.com/collections/all?sort_by=best-selling

The store will refresh and sort products in order of sales volume. Open the **Exnta extension** popup on that page and click "Download Products" to export the catalog in that exact order—instantly revealing their top-performing products.
