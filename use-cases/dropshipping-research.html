Streamline Your Dropshipping Product Research

Find margins, spy on hot inventories, and construct high-converting catalog spreadsheets in seconds.

For dropshippers, success relies on speed and data. To stand out, you must use **shopify competitor analysis tools** to understand your competitors' pricing models, the variety of variants they carry, and the wholesale margins available.

Instead of copying products manually one-by-one, **Shopify Product Exporter** allows you to audit any Shopify competitor store. Download their entire catalog into styled Excel tables or import-ready CSVs in one click.

Dropshipping Research Workflow:

  1. Identify Competitors: Locate successful Shopify dropshipping stores in your niche.
  2. Scrape Catalog Data: Open the extension on the store and click "Download Products" to save their titles, prices, descriptions, and variant configurations.
  3. Analyze & Optimize: Open the spreadsheet in Excel to review pricing margins, compare-at discounts, and locate high-res supplier images.
  4. Bulk Upload: Import the formatted CSV into your own store dashboard, ready for inventory configuration.

Why Shopify Product Exporter is the Best Dropshipping Companion:

  • No API Key Needed: Scrape public product pages directly without requesting credentials.
  • Safe & Sandboxed: Runs locally on your browser. Safe from IP blocks that affect standard cloud scrapers.
  • Bulk Image Downloader: Download high-resolution images in batch, with automatic handle-based naming to preserve SEO.
