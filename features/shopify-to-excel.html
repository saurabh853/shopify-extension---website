Why Choose Excel (.xlsx) Over CSV?

Excel is the premier tool for financial modeling, pricing comparison, and visual reporting. Excel downloads bypass standard text parsing issues.

While CSV is great for database uploads, opening raw CSV files in Microsoft Excel often results in formatting errors. Common issues include leading zeros being removed from SKUs, numbers formatted as scientific notation, and broken text encodings (UTF-8) for international characters.

The **Shopify Product Exporter's** Excel exporter solves this by writing binary `.xlsx` files directly from the browser. It applies proper datatypes to cells, preventing spreadsheet programs from corrupting your product SKUs, option names, or pricing floats.

Key Benefits of our Excel Exporter:

  • Styled Headers & Auto-Widths: Columns automatically resize to fit your data, featuring professional, readable grid headers.
  • Variant worksheets: Easily **export shopify variants to excel** with multi-variant products mapped to separate rows or worksheets, keeping parent listings distinct from child options (Size, Color, Material).
  • Formula Ready: All prices are exported as numeric values, allowing you to instantly run SUM, AVERAGE, and custom profit margin formulas. If you also need to **extract shopify products to csv**, our tool supports fast format switching.
