Why Export Shopify Products to CSV?

CSV is the universal language of e-commerce databases. It allows you to move catalog lists cleanly between tools.

When conducting product analysis or setting up new online storefronts, manually copying product attributes is slow and tedious. If you need to export shopify store to csv files, using our free "Shopify Product Exporter" extension allows you to download all catalog data instantly.

Our exported CSV structure uses standard UTF-8 text formatting and complies with Shopify's default headers. This means you can import the exported files directly back into your own Shopify or WooCommerce dashboards without mapping columns manually.

Ideal for:

  • E-commerce Migrations: Moving from Shopify to WooCommerce or backing up product listings.
  • Dropshipping Audits: Pulling competitor product models and variant descriptions for suppliers.
  • Data Cleanups: Editing prices and SKUs in bulk in Excel or Google Sheets, then re-uploading.
