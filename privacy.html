Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 7, 2026

1. Information Collection and Usage

We want to assure our users that Shopify Product Exporter is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect personal information, and we respect your right to privacy. We utilize a third-party service named Lemon Squeezy for payment processing, and we are committed to ensuring that your data is always handled with the utmost care and respect.

2. Data Collection

No personal information: Shopify Product Exporter does not collect personally identifiable information, browsing history, or user preferences.

3. Third-Party Payment Processing

Payment transactions are processed through Lemon Squeezy for users opting for premium features. Please refer to Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy for specific details about the data they collect during the payment process.

4. How Shopify Product Exporter Works

Shopify Product Exporter operates solely on the local browser. It does not send or receive data from external servers apart from the necessary communication with Lemon Squeezy during payment, which is essential for processing your premium feature subscription.

5. User Consent

By using Shopify Product Exporter, users acknowledge and agree to use Lemon Squeezy for payment processing and understand that Lemon Squeezy has its own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

While Shopify Product Exporter does not directly handle payment information, we rely on Lemon Squeezy's secure payment infrastructure. Users' payment information is subject to Lemon Squeezy's security measures.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

At Exnta, we believe in transparency. Any future updates to our privacy policy will be clearly communicated to our users. Any changes related to the use of Lemon Squeezy will be promptly reflected in this document, ensuring that you are always informed and empowered.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, the operation of Shopify Product Exporter, or the payment process through Lemon Squeezy, please contact us at:

support@exnta.com
