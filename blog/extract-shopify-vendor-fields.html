How to Extract Vendor Brand Fields for Directory Building

Building a directory of distributors or manufacturers in your niche is a great way to map out suppliers. While most store designs display unified branding on the frontend, the underlying store databases often store the name of the original vendor or supplier. Here is how to extract Shopify vendor fields for database building.

Vetting Competitor Supply Lines

Analyzing vendor fields helps you:

  • Identify Sourcing Partners: Group products by supplier handles to locate the original manufacturers.
  • Build Wholesale Catalogs: Map out brand availability lists to plan your custom product range.
  • Streamline Sourcing: Match competitor catalog listings to major wholesale supplier directories.

Automating Sourcing Audits

Using local browser extensions allows you to extract vendor fields from any public Shopify storefront. The tool exports all vendor names to a structured spreadsheet column, ready to support your supplier research.
