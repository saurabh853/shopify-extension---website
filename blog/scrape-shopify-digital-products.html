How to Scrape Shopify Gift Card Data and Digital Products

Many e-commerce brands sell intangible items like gift cards, software, eBooks, or courses. Because these products do not require physical shipping, their database attributes (such as shipping weights or fulfillment requirements) differ from physical products. Understanding how to scrape Shopify digital products helps you catalog digital catalogs accurately.

Attributes of Digital Product Listings

Digital products feature specific attributes in the store database:

  • Requires Shipping: Set to `false` to prevent the checkout page from requesting shipping details.
  • Tax Settings: Configured differently depending on local tax regulations for digital items.
  • Fulfillment Settings: Connected to digital delivery apps rather than physical shipping lines.

Extracting Digital Products Natively

Using a browser extension allows you to extract these digital product attributes. The exported spreadsheet will list variant details and configuration flags, helping you design a clean import sheet for your own digital catalog.
