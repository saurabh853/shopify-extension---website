Tracking E-Commerce Launch Velocity: Who is adding items fastest?

Agility is a key success factor in fast-moving niches like fashion or beauty. Brands that test new trends quickly are more likely to capture rising search volume. Tracking e-commerce launch velocity allows you to monitor how quickly competitors add and test new products in their catalogs.

Measuring Launch Speed

To audit launch velocity, export competitor catalogs regularly. By comparing chronological listings, you can calculate the number of new products they add over a fixed time frame. This data shows which competitors are expanding their ranges fastest, helping you adjust your launch speed to match.

Using Shopify Product Exporter

Exporting catalogs locally allows you to audit these launch patterns without triggering IP blocks, providing accurate datasets to support your competitive research.
