Extracting Product Create Dates from Shopify Store Maps

Tracking when a competitor launches new products gives you valuable insight into their strategy. If they launch multiple products in a subcategory, it suggests they are expanding a profitable line. You can find Shopify product creation dates by inspecting the store's public XML sitemaps.

Finding Creation Dates in XML Sitemaps

Shopify automatically generates sitemaps at `/sitemap.xml`. Opening the product sitemap link (such as `/sitemap_products_1.xml`) displays a list of active product pages along with their `lastmod` (last modified) dates. This helps you identify which products are updated regularly.

Using Browser Extensions

To analyze launch timing at scale, you can use local tools to extract creation dates and modified dates. This metadata allows you to calculate product launch frequencies and map out your competitor's operational schedule.
