Moving Heavy Catalog Architecture from Shopify to CoreCommerce

Migrate Shopify to CoreCommerce is the enterprise data process of transferring large-scale product catalogs, categories, and inventory configurations from Shopify to CoreCommerce using formatted CSV sheets.

Enterprise merchants often migrate to CoreCommerce to utilize its advanced multi-storefront and custom checkout features. Shifting a large catalog with multiple variants requires careful planning to prevent data loss or broken listings.

Mapping CoreCommerce Catalog Columns

CoreCommerce uses structured catalog headers. Map your exported Shopify columns to these CoreCommerce fields:

  • Modify `Title` to `Product Name`.
  • Map `Variant SKU` to `SKU`.
  • Map `Variant Price` to `Price`.
  • Verify variant attributes align with CoreCommerce's option configurations.

Executing the Migration

First, use a local browser tool to export shopify products to csv cleanly. Clean up formatting anomalies in your editor, and import the file to CoreCommerce to complete the catalog transfer.
