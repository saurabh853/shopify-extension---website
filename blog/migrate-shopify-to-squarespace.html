Replatforming Guide: Shifting from Shopify to Squarespace

Migrate Shopify to Squarespace is the technical workflow of exporting product listings, options, and media assets from Shopify and importing them into a Squarespace store.

Squarespace is favored by creative brands for its elegant, design-focused templates. If you are shifting your store catalog from Shopify to Squarespace, exporting your products as a spreadsheet allows you to populate your new Squarespace collections quickly.

Squarespace Data Layout Requirements

To ensure a smooth import, adjust your columns to match Squarespace's required template:

  • `Product Type`: Set to `Physical` or `Digital`.
  • `Product Page`: Specify the target store collection path.
  • `Description`: Map from the Shopify `Body (HTML)` column.
  • `Stock`: Map from the Shopify `Variant Inventory Qty` column.

Executing the Import

Export your catalog cleanly using Shopify Product Exporter. Check column headings in your editor, and import the file directly to Squarespace to complete the setup.
