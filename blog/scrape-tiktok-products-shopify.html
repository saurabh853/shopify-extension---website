How to Scrape Tik Tok Viral Products From Shopify Stores

TikTok is the ultimate engine for viral e-commerce products. When an item goes viral on TikTok, dozens of fast-moving Shopify stores quickly list it to capture search traffic and impulse buys. To keep up with these trends, dropshippers need to scrape TikTok viral products from competitor sites to compare prices and source them quickly.

In this tutorial, we show you how to extract trending TikTok product listings locally using browser extension tools.

1. Spotting Viral Items on Social Feeds

Monitor hashtags like `#TikTokMadeMeBuyIt` or `#ShopifyFinds` on TikTok. Note down the stores that are running ads or getting millions of organic views on their showcase pages.

2. Exporting Competitor Collections

Navigate to the store's main catalog page (typically `/collections/all`). Use a browser extension like Shopify Product Exporter to export their entire active catalog. This saves you from copying and pasting titles, pricing models, and variant options manually.

3. Mapping the Sourcing Details

With the exported data in a CSV or Excel sheet, you can quickly reverse engineer the product. Search the image URLs or titles on supplier sites to locate the exact manufacturing lines and secure lower unit costs.
