Rebuilding an Extracted Store Blueprint inside Webflow E-Commerce

Migrate Shopify to Webflow is the technical catalog process of exporting product listings and variations from Shopify and loading them into Webflow's E-Commerce CMS fields.

Designers often choose Webflow E-Commerce to create custom web designs. If you are moving your catalog from Shopify to Webflow, exporting your product data as a spreadsheet allows you to populate your Webflow CMS collections in bulk.

Webflow CMS Column Mapping Rules

Webflow uses a specific CMS structure. Map your exported Shopify columns to these Webflow fields:

  • Modify `Title` to `Name`.
  • Map `Body (HTML)` to `Description`.
  • Map `Variant Price` to `Price`.
  • Map `Image Src` to `Main Image`.

Running the Database Shift

First, use a local browser tool to export shopify products to csv cleanly. Clean up the sheet headers in your editor, and import the file to Webflow E-Commerce to build your product collection.
