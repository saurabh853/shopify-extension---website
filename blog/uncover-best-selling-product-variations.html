How to Uncover a Brand’s Secret Best-Selling Variations

When a product has multiple options (like colors or sizes), sales are rarely distributed evenly. Typically, a single variation (such as the black option or size medium) drives the majority of the product's sales volume. You can uncover a brand's best-selling variations to avoid wasting ad spend on lower-converting options.

Why Variant Analysis is Crucial

Focusing on popular variations offers several benefits:

  • Optimize Sourcing Budgets: Focus your capital on importing the specific variations that convert best.
  • Reduce Advertising Costs: Direct your ad budgets to promote the highest-converting variations.
  • Prevent Slow-Moving Stock: Avoid inventory build-ups of slow-selling colors or sizes.

Extracting Variant Popularity Natively

Using local browser extensions allows you to export variant metadata and check inventory change speeds. This helps you identify which product configurations generate the highest sales volume.
