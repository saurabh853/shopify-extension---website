Shifting Store Listings to eBay: Formating variant option grids

Convert Shopify Catalog to eBay is the data formatting process of converting Shopify product spreadsheets into the CSV templates required by eBay's Merchant Integration Platform.

Selling your products on eBay is a great way to expand your e-commerce channels. However, eBay uses a unique catalog structure that differs from Shopify's layout. Restructuring your spreadsheet columns allows you to import your listings and options in bulk.

Mapping eBay Catalog Fields

Format your spreadsheet columns to match eBay's required layout:

  • Modify `Title` to `Action(SiteID=US|Country=US|Currency=USD|Version=745)`.
  • Map `Variant SKU` to `Custom Label (SKU)`.
  • Map `Variant Price` to `Start Price`.
  • Verify variant attributes align with eBay's item specificity rules.

Uploading the Data Sheet

First, use a local extension to export shopify to excel cleanly. Clean up formatting anomalies in your editor, and import the file to eBay to build your listings.
