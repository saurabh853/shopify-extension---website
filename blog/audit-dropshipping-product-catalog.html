How to Audit a Competitor’s Dropshipping Product Catalog

To scale your own brand, you must understand your competition. Auditing a competitor's setup reveals their best-selling price ranges, search keywords, and variant choices. Running a dropshipping product catalog audit manually is tedious, but you can complete it quickly with the right workflow.

Here is our quick workflow for auditing competitor catalogs in under 5 minutes.

1. Scrape the Catalog to Spreadsheet Format

Open the target store, load the main catalog page, and use Shopify Product Exporter to extract all product lines to an Excel sheet. This captures product titles, descriptions, compare-at pricing levels, image counts, and variant structures.

2. Perform Variant and Pricing Audits

In Excel, calculate the average price, check if they offer volume discounts, and look for missing variant options (like sizes or colors) that you can offer to provide a better shopping experience for customers.
