How to Spy on Shopify Stores in the Fashion & Apparel Niche

The fashion and apparel niche is a highly competitive segment of e-commerce. Because apparel relies heavily on color choices, sizes, and seasonal lines, catalog databases in this niche are complex. Learning how to spy on fashion Shopify stores helps you map out these variations and plan your own catalog strategy.

Handling Variant Grids in Excel

Fashion listings often feature a high number of variants (e.g. Size: S/M/L, Color: Blue/Green). When you export this data, each product will span multiple variant rows. You need to map these rows correctly to protect option relationships in your import files.
