How to Export Shopify Inventory Levels and SKUs

Proper inventory tracking is essential to prevent overselling, manage supplier orders, and coordinate fulfillment operations. In Shopify, inventory levels are tracked at the individual variant level. Every product variant is assigned a Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) and a stock quantity value.

Whether you're running audits, performing pricing audits, or preparing catalog records for external warehouse management systems, exporting your SKUs and stock quantities regularly is highly recommended. In this guide, we explain how to extract this data securely.

Why SKU and Stock Exporting is Crucial

Maintaining a clean, updated record of your inventory data is useful for:

  • Supplier Ordering Coordination: Easily build product restocking lists by sorting variants in Excel to identify items falling below safety stock limits.
  • Warehouse Syncing: Match physical warehouse inventory sheets with SKU lists to correct database entry discrepancies.
  • Dropshipping Tracking: Sync pricing lists and stock codes with supplier databases to keep listings up to date.

Method: 1-Click Inventory Export via Shopify Product Exporter

To pull inventory details directly without installing heavy inventory control software:

  1. Install the Shopify Product Exporter Chrome extension.
  2. Open the Shopify Admin Panel or your target store and click the extension icon.
  3. Click **Download Products**.
  4. Export the dataset as an **Excel Workbook** (`.xlsx`).
  5. Open the file and check the **Variants** sheet. The extension populates: * **Variant SKU:** The specific stock tracking code. * **Variant Inventory Tracker:** Indicates whether Shopify tracks the variant's stock. * **Variant Inventory Qty:** Displays the active stock quantity available for that specific variant.

Handling Variant Barcodes

In addition to SKUs, the sheet exports the *Variant Barcode* (UPC/EAN) column if barcodes are configured in the database, allowing you to sync catalog data with Amazon, eBay, or Google Merchant Center.

Conclusion

Exporting inventory quantities and SKUs is straightforward when using a direct browser-based extraction tool. With **Shopify Product Exporter**, you get a structured Excel sheet containing variant-level stock parameters, simplifying inventory audits and database syncs.
