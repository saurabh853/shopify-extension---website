Exporting Multi-Language Variant Layers out of Shopify Stores

Export Multi-Language Shopify Store Data is the development process of extracting localized product translations, descriptions, and regional price variations from multilingual Shopify stores.

Shopify stores often use localization apps to translate listings for international markets. When auditing or migrating these stores, you need to extract localized variant layers without breaking catalog structures. Here is how to crawl localized Shopify layouts.

Accessing Translated Catalog Fields

Multilingual stores serve translated layouts under localized URLs (such as `/es/products` or `/fr/products`). To capture translations:

  • Query Localized Endpoints: Check if you can access product metadata by appending localized paths to the root domain.
  • Extract Regional Price Variations: Track currency scaling setups across regional variants.

Extracting Localized Catalogs Safely

Using a browser extension allows you to extract localized metadata from the browser window memory. The tool exports all details to structured files, ready to support your multi-language catalogs.
