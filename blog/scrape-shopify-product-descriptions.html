The Dropshipper’s Guide to Scraping Product Descriptions

Writing high-converting product descriptions from scratch is time-consuming, especially when testing large catalogs. While you should never copy content directly, you can scrape product descriptions from top competitors to use as structural templates for your copywriters or AI rewriting tools.

This guide explains how to extract clean text descriptions from competitor catalogs in bulk.

1. The Problem with Manual Copy-Pasting

Copying descriptions manually from browser windows often copies hidden HTML styling, font tags, and tracking codes. This can break your store's theme design when you paste them into your editor.

2. Exporting Clean Text Data

Exporting catalog listings using browser extensions extracts the description text cleanly. This structured data can then be imported directly into translation tools or AI text rewriters to create unique descriptions that match your brand's voice.
