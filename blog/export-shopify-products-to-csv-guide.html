How to Export Shopify Products to CSV: The Complete Guide

Exporting your product catalog is a regular task for e-commerce operators. Whether you are migrating to a new storefront, creating backups, or analyzing margins, downloading product databases is essential. Many merchants believe they need API keys or custom apps to perform this task, but you can export Shopify products to CSV natively and safely.

The Default Shopify Export Limits

Shopify allows store owners to export catalogs directly from the admin panel. However, this method has limitations:

  • Email Delays: If your catalog contains more than 50 items, Shopify emails you a zip file, which can take up to an hour to arrive.
  • Formatting Issues: Large catalogs frequently fail to export cleanly, resulting in corrupted descriptions or truncated image URLs.
  • Account Access Needed: You must have full administrative store privileges to access the backend exporter.

Exporting Catalogs Locally in 1-Click

If you need to export catalog data quickly, you can use local extraction tools like Shopify Product Exporter. The extension extracts complete product lists, variants, image CDN paths, and tag structures directly from the storefront DOM, bypassing backend admin panels entirely.
