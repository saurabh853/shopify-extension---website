How to Isolate and Scrape New Product Arrivals Weekly

Monitoring competitor launches over time can provide useful competitive insights. If you notice a store routinely adding products to a specific category, it suggests they are seeing solid results in that segment. Setting up a routine to track new Shopify arrivals weekly helps you identify these opportunities early.

Setting Up Your Weekly Sourcing Audit

  1. Create a list of 5-10 top competitor stores in your niche.
  2. Every Monday, visit their collection pages and sort by new arrivals (e.g. `?sort_by=created-descending`).
  3. Use Shopify Product Exporter to extract the first few pages of recent items.
  4. Compare the new items against previous exports to identify their latest additions.

Adapting to Market Trends

Tracking product launch frequencies helps you identify emerging trends and adjust your own catalog and marketing strategy to match user interest.
