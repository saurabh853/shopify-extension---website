Exporting Shopify Metadata arrays into Shoplazza Layouts

Migrate Shopify to Shoplazza is the technical catalog process of exporting product listings, options, and media assets from Shopify and importing them into a Shoplazza store.

Shoplazza is favored by merchants targeting international sales due to its strong multi-currency features. If you are moving your catalog from Shopify to Shoplazza, exporting your product data as a spreadsheet allows you to populate your Shoplazza collections quickly.

Shoplazza Data Layout Requirements

To ensure a smooth import, adjust your columns to match Shoplazza's required template:

  • Modify `Title` to `Product Name`.
  • Map `Variant SKU` to `SKU`.
  • Map `Variant Price` to `Price`.
  • Map `Image Src` to `Main Image`.

Executing the Import

First, use a local browser tool to export shopify products to csv cleanly. Check column headings in your editor, and import the file directly to Shoplazza to complete the setup.
