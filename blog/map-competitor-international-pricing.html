How to Map a Competitor’s International Pricing Strategies

Selling products internationally requires a localized pricing strategy. Competitors may adjust their prices in regional markets to account for local tax rates, shipping costs, or purchasing power. Learning how to map competitor international pricing helps you optimize your cross-border pricing model.

Auditing Regional Price Structures

To inspect regional pricing, visit the target store using a VPN or currency selectors and export the catalog for each target country. Reviewing these price points side-by-side reveals their international markups and exchange rate models.

Optimizing International Margins

Mapping international pricing structures gives you the data you need to set profitable price points in different countries, helping you scale your global sales volume.
