How to Download Specific Collections from a Shopify Store

Auditing a competitor's entire store can generate a lot of data that is difficult to process, especially for large catalogs. If you only want to analyze a single product category (like shoes, bags, or chargers), you can save time by learning how to download specific collections from Shopify.

Focusing Your Competitor Audits

Targeting specific collections offers several benefits:

  • Save Processing Time: Avoid exporting irrelevant product categories, keeping your files clean and focused.
  • Analyze Specific Trends: Focus your research on high-performing product categories.
  • Speed Up Sourcing: Create clear sourcing lists for specific suppliers or product lines.

Exporting Selected Collections

To export a single collection, navigate to that collection page in your browser and use a local tool like Shopify Product Exporter. The extension will extract only the products in that collection, saving the data to a clean spreadsheet.
