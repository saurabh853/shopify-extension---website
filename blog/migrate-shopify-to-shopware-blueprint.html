Shifting Catalogs From Shopify to Shopware: Column Naming Blueprint

Migrate Shopify to Shopware is the database migration process of exporting product lists and variations from Shopify and mapping them to match Shopware's multi-tier localization database.

Shopware is commonly used by European brands to manage international pricing and localized catalogs. Shifting your catalog from Shopify to Shopware requires matching columns to align with Shopware's database layout.

Shopware Layout Configuration

Map your columns to match Shopware's required fields:

  • `productNumber`: Map from the Shopify `Variant SKU` column.
  • `name`: Map from the Shopify `Title` column.
  • `price`: Map from `Variant Price` (must be formatted as a JSON array to specify tax levels).
  • `stock`: Map from `Variant Inventory Qty`.

Executing the Migration

Export your Shopify products using a local extension. Modify the column headers in your editor, and import the file to Shopware to complete the catalog transfer.
