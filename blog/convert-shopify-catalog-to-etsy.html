Shifting to Marketplace Frameworks: Shopify to Etsy Listing Guidelines

Convert Shopify Catalog to Etsy is the data formatting process of converting Shopify product spreadsheets into the CSV templates required by Etsy's bulk listing manager.

Selling your products on Etsy is a great way to reach creative buyers. However, Etsy uses a unique catalog structure that differs from Shopify's layout. Restructuring your spreadsheet columns allows you to import your listings and options in bulk.

Mapping Etsy Catalog Fields

Format your spreadsheet columns to match Etsy's required layout:

  • Modify `Title` to `Title *`.
  • Map `Variant Price` to `Price *`.
  • Map `Variant Inventory Qty` to `Quantity *`.
  • Verify variant attributes align with Etsy's handmade listing guidelines.

Uploading the Data Sheet

First, use a browser tool to dropshipping product research and export the catalog. Clean up formatting anomalies in your editor, and import the file to Etsy to build your listings.
