How to See Competitors Shopify Products and Best-Sellers

When launching new products or setting up dropshipping lines, you want to focus on items that have already proven to sell. Inspecting competitor storefronts is a reliable way to source ideas. While store dashboards are private, you can see competitors' Shopify products and best-sellers using simple platform methods.

Querying Best-Seller Feeds

By default, Shopify has sorting mechanisms built into its collection layouts. Adding `?sort_by=best-selling` to a store's `/collections/all` catalog URL instructs the system to list products in order of sales volume. This places their top-performing items at the top of the collection grid.

Extracting Best-Seller Lists

Once you load the sorted page, you can export the listings to a spreadsheet. Having this list in Excel or CSV format makes it easy to analyze their best-selling lines and plan your sourcing checklist.
