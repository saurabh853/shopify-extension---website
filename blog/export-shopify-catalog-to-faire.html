Moving Wholesale Inventories from Shopify to Faire Directory

Export Shopify Catalog to Faire is the B2B catalog process of converting direct-to-consumer Shopify spreadsheets into the CSV templates required by the Faire wholesale marketplace.

Selling wholesale on Faire is a popular way to expand your B2B sales. To build your wholesale catalog without manual entry, you can export your products as a spreadsheet and format the columns to match Faire's template.

Faire Catalog Mapping Rules

Adjust your exported Shopify data columns to match Faire's template:

  • Map `Title` to `Product Name`.
  • Map `Variant SKU` to `SKU`.
  • Map `Variant Price` to `Retail Price`, and define a B2B column for your wholesale price.
  • Ensure variant attributes align with Faire's option configurations.

Running the Integration

Export your Shopify products using a local extension. Modify the column headers in your editor, and import the file to your Faire dashboard to build your wholesale listings.
