Summary

When to Choose Each Tool

Choose Shopify Product Exporter When:

You need fast, private product data exports (CSV, Excel) from Shopify stores without creating accounts, sharing API keys, or sending data to external servers. Ideal for dropshippers doing competitor research, merchants migrating to WooCommerce, and developers extracting structured product data. Shopify Product Exporter is the most cost-effective option at $3.99/month for unlimited Pro features.

Choose Koala Inspector When:

You primarily need Shopify theme detection, installed app analysis, and general store auditing capabilities. Koala focuses on store inspection rather than data export, making it complementary to (not a replacement for) a dedicated product exporter.

Choose AliHunter When:

You are sourcing products specifically from AliExpress and need supplier matching, order volume tracking, and China-based supply chain tools. AliHunter is a sourcing-first tool, not a general Shopify scraper.

Choose PPSPY When:

You need real-time sales estimation, store traffic analysis, and ad tracking for competitor intelligence at scale. PPSPY is the most expensive option ($29.99/month) but provides the broadest competitive intelligence dashboard.
